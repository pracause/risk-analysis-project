# Corporate Risk Analysis: Credit, Liquidity and Market Risk

End-to-end risk analysis of a plastics and chemicals manufacturer, using Excel (Power Query), MySQL and Power BI.

**Headline:** Industrial Resins is the weakest of five business units on all three risks: 55% of its receivables are overdue, its 30-day cash cover fell to 0.55, and 78% of its crude-oil purchases are unhedged.

## Data

* **Real:** daily USD/INR, USD per EUR and WTI crude prices from [FRED](https://fred.stlouisfed.org) (`DEXINUS`, `DEXUSEU`, `DCOILWTICO`), April 2023 to March 2025. EUR/INR is derived.
* **Synthetic:** customers, invoices, purchases, treasury and business units for a fictional company, generated by a Python script (fixed seed) with AI assistance, with data-quality problems injected on purpose. The findings demonstrate method, not evidence about real companies.

## Pipeline

`Raw CSV → Excel Power Query (cleaning) → Excel formulas and pivots → MySQL queries and Power BI dashboard`

## Process and technical functions

### 1\. Excel (Power Query and formulas)

|Step|What I did|Functions and features used|
|-|-|-|
|Clean|Fixed about 35 types of data problem across 6 tables: duplicates, stray spaces, inconsistent labels (country, currency, status), numbers stored as text, dates in three formats, impossible values (negative amounts, payment before invoice), a price decimal slip, broken keys (12 rows quarantined), cash that did not reconcile. Row counts reconciled (invoices 2,745 → 2,688).|Trim, Remove Duplicates, Replace Values, UPPERCASE and Capitalize Each Word, Change Type using Locale, Replace Errors, Fill Down, Custom Column in M (`Text.Select`, `Number.FromText`, `Number.Min`, `Number.Round`), flag columns, row filters, column profiling|
|Add columns|Put USD, EUR and INR on one rupee scale and measured overdue and payment speed|`INDEX` with `MATCH` (approximate match), `SUMIFS`, `IF`, `OR`, `MAX`, `DATE`, nested `IF` for aging buckets|
|Analyse|Aging of unpaid invoices, volatility and VaR, cash conversion cycle|PivotTable, `STDEV.S`, `PERCENTILE.INC`, `SQRT`, `AVERAGEIFS`, `SUMIFS` (DSO, DIO, DPO)|
|Check|Control totals before any analysis|`COUNTIF`, `SUMPRODUCT` with `ISERROR`, row-count reconciliation|

## Questions, tools and findings

|Q|Question|Tool and key functions|Finding|
|-|-|-|-|
|1|Which unit has the most overdue receivables?|SQL: `JOIN`, `SUM` with `CASE WHEN`, `GROUP BY`|Industrial Resins: 55% overdue, against 18% to 32% elsewhere|
|2|Do weaker-rated customers hold most of it?|SQL: `COUNT(DISTINCT …)`, conditional `SUM`|B, BB and CCC are 27% of customers and hold 62% of overdue money|
|3|Who are the largest overdue customers?|SQL: `WHERE`, `ORDER BY`, `LIMIT`|The top 10 owe ₹42.9 cr (46%), all rated BB or worse or unrated|
|4|Who owes more than their credit limit?|SQL: `HAVING`|17 customers exceed their limit by ₹50.7 cr; 4 customers with invalid limits owe ₹15.5 cr|
|5|How old are the unpaid invoices?|Excel: PivotTable, nested `IF`|₹43.6 cr (47% of overdue) is over 90 days old|
|6|Which units came closest to a cash squeeze?|SQL: `MIN`, `AVG`, conditional count|Industrial Resins was below 1.0 in four months (low 0.55)|
|7|How strong is working capital?|SQL: `AVG` of ratios|Quick ratio from 1.50 (Industrial Resins) to 2.45 (Packaging Films)|
|8|How volatile are prices, and what is the worst one-day move?|Excel: `STDEV.S`, `PERCENTILE.INC`|Oil 29.5% a year against 2.5% for USD/INR|
|9|How much crude buying is unhedged, and did hedging pay?|SQL: `WHERE`, conditional `SUM`|54% unhedged (78% in Industrial Resins); hedges cost $1.38m as oil fell|
|10|How much foreign-currency money to collect is unhedged?|SQL: `IN`, several `WHERE` conditions, two-column `GROUP BY`|₹59.7 cr unhedged, led by the euro (₹31.0 cr)|
|11|How many days is cash tied up?|Excel: `AVERAGEIFS`, `SUMIFS`|92 to 104 days, driven by slow customer payment|

## Recommendations

1. A recovery plan for Industrial Resins covering collections, hedging and a cash buffer.
2. Lower limits and shorter terms for customers rated B and below, and block orders when a limit is breached.
3. A collections push on balances over 90 days and on the top ten overdue customers.
4. A minimum hedging policy for crude oil and euro receivables.
5. Fix invalid credit limits and monitor cash cover monthly.

## Limitations

Synthetic data; assumed red/amber/green limits (overdue 30% or more, cover below 1.0, crude unhedged 60% or more); DIO uses purchases in place of cost of goods; DSO leaves out invoices more than 180 days past due; stress tests ignore offsetting gains.
